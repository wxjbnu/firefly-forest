# Firefly Forest V2 Style

## Core Identity

Firefly Forest is a gentle night-forest collecting game. The visual identity is calm, readable, and warm: moonlit green foliage, soft golden lantern light, quiet blue shadows, and small glowing insects that feel magical rather than noisy.

## Art Direction

- Style: polished anime-inspired mobile game art with soft painterly lighting.
- Palette: deep moss green, blue-black night, warm gold, mint green, cool firefly blue.
- Shape language: rounded, soft, organic silhouettes; no sharp horror shapes.
- Readability: clear center play area, high contrast sprites, no embedded text.
- UI: lantern-and-wood motif, warm rim light, transparent PNG-friendly edges.

## Resource Rules

- Backgrounds should be portrait mobile, with the playable center kept open.
- Fireflies should be single centered sprites with large readable glow abdomen.
- UI panels and buttons must have no text and enough empty center space for labels.
- Transparent assets should be generated on flat chroma-key background before alpha cleanup.
