# Firefly Forest

A firefly catching game for iOS where you guide a glowing lantern through a dark forest arena to catch fireflies of matching colors.

## Gameplay

Guide your glowing lantern through a darkened forest arena. Your lantern emits light in one of three colors: Gold, Green, or Blue. Tap the color chips at the bottom of the screen to switch your lantern's light color, then drag through the play area to catch matching fireflies. Each firefly within your lantern's 42-point catch radius and of the matching color is captured.

Fireflies of different colors bounce around the arena in all directions. Timing your catches and planning your path through the swarm is essential to meeting each level's target within the time limit.

Each catch scores 10 base points multiplied by your catch count. Consecutive catches of the same color within 2.5 seconds build a combo bonus of an additional 5 points per extra catch, rewarding fast, focused play.

From Area 3 onward, rare golden fireflies may appear with an 8% spawn chance. They are caught only with the Gold lantern and award a +50 point bonus when captured.

The game features 50 areas with increasing difficulty. Each new area introduces more fireflies, shorter time limits, and a higher catch target. Areas 1-2 use only two colors, while Area 3 and beyond activate all three, raising the challenge significantly.

Your progress is saved locally. Unlocked areas remain accessible between sessions, letting you return to master difficult levels at your own pace.
